# # Excel Sales Performance Dashboard | Business Case Study

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyses sales performance across products, categories, countries, and months using Microsoft Excel.

The objective is to transform raw sales data into actionable business insights that help stakeholders understand revenue performance, profitability, product demand, and potential growth opportunities.

Rather than focusing only on visualisation, this project explores how sales data can support business analysis and decision-making.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Evaluate overall revenue and gross profit performance.
- Identify top-performing product categories.
- Analyse monthly revenue and gross profit trends.
- Compare revenue contribution across countries.
- Identify high-demand products using units sold.
- Develop recommendations for further business investigation.

## 🛠️ Tools & Techniques

- **Microsoft Excel**
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Interactive Slicers and Date Timeline
- KPI Cards
- Bar Charts and Line Charts
- Geographic Map Visualisation
- Business Insight Generation

## 🔍 Key Business Findings

### 1. Product Category Performance

| Category | Revenue |
|---|---:|
| Camping | $198,505.13 |
| Hiking | $179,959.14 |
| Travel | $123,724.49 |
| Cycling | $87,481.99 |
| Fitness | $55,552.43 |

**Business interpretation:** Camping and Hiking are the largest revenue-generating categories in this dataset.

**Recommended next step:** Compare gross margin, sales growth and inventory turnover across categories before deciding where to increase investment.

### 2. Monthly Revenue Trends

- June recorded the highest monthly revenue at approximately $70K.
- February recorded approximately $36K.
- Revenue recovered after February and reached another strong level in November.

**Business interpretation:** Monthly performance varies considerably.

**Recommended next step:** Investigate seasonality, discounts, stock availability and campaign performance to understand the drivers behind monthly changes.

### 3. Geographic Performance

| Country | Revenue |
|---|---:|
| United Kingdom | $74,427.37 |
| Germany | $70,034.11 |
| France | $55,102.05 |
| Italy | $50,319.47 |
| Spain | $46,624.59 |

**Business interpretation:** The United Kingdom and Germany are the leading revenue-generating countries in the dataset.

**Recommended next step:** Compare market-level profitability, customer retention and acquisition costs before prioritising expansion.

### 4. Product Demand Analysis

| Product | Units Sold |
|---|---:|
| Trail Boots | 449 |
| Summit Backpack 45L | 447 |
| Compact Sleeping Bag | 433 |
| Hiking Daypack 20L | 396 |
| Trail Bike Light | 392 |

**Business interpretation:** Trail Boots and Summit Backpack 45L lead this selection in units sold.

**Recommended next step:** Evaluate product margins, inventory levels and complementary product purchases to identify replenishment and cross-selling opportunities.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the initial analysis, the next areas of investigation would be:

1. **Profitability analysis:** Identify which products and countries contribute the most gross profit.
2. **Seasonality analysis:** Understand monthly revenue fluctuations and improve sales planning.
3. **Customer analysis:** Compare new and returning customers to investigate retention opportunities.
4. **Channel analysis:** Evaluate sales performance across Corporate, Marketplace, Online Store and Retail Partners.
5. **Inventory planning:** Use product demand patterns to support replenishment decisions.

These are proposed areas for further analysis, not confirmed causes of the observed performance.
